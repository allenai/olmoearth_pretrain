BlueTopo Depth Probe

Does training OlmoEarth on water depth help, and at what cost? Six tiny models: two v1.2 (base, and trained toward a satellite-derived-depth formula) and four v1.3 that differ only in which depth targets reach the encoder. Depth is scored against NOAA's BlueTopo survey grids, only on the 30% of 1° cells that no model saw BlueTopo labels for. Vessel and seagrass tasks check for side effects.

Each dot is a depth-trained model minus its control on one task: the three v1.3 depth-supervised models against the v1.3 probe-only model (same architecture, depth head detached from the encoder), and v1.2 sdb against v1.2 base. Setup and caveats

  • Labels: BlueTopo elevation (2–8 m tiles) averaged onto each sample's 10 m UTM grid, then to 40 m tokens. A token counts if all 16 pixels are valid and below 0 m and its mean depth is 1–30 m. Grid alignment was checked against Sentinel-2 water: agreement peaks at zero shift.
  • Samples: pretraining samples whose centre is in a BlueTopo tile and that contain 0–30 m water according to GEBCO. About 4% were skipped because their tile version had been replaced on S3 (404).
  • Features: S2 L2A only, all available months, patch size 4. v1.2 models use encoder tokens mean-pooled over time; v1.3 models use the attention-pooler location tokens, which is what the in-loop evals probe.
  • Probe: standardised ridge, alpha chosen by GCV on the training fold. Five folds grouped by 1° cell. Differences between arms come with a 95% bootstrap interval over cells.
  • Held-out cells: 30% of the 1° cells containing a BlueTopo sample (seed 0) got no BlueTopo training label. Everything on this page except the all-cells table is scored on those cells only: 36 cells after dropping coarse tiles. The two NOAA-supervised models saw BlueTopo labels in the other 70%.
  • The imagery is in the pretraining set, and the sdb models saw formula-derived depth targets on it. A version on held-out imagery would need new Sentinel-2 windows.
  • Vessel: OlmoEarth eval tasks small_sentinel2_vessel_type and _length, linear probe, best val. Seagrass: the rslearn Balearics pipeline from round 1, with the depth heads stripped from each bundle (encoder output unchanged).
